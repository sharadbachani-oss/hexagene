2 · The required attachment — Sensitive Data Access Use Agreement

I could not retrieve this template for you — this environment's network policy blocks hrs.isr.umich.edu. But you can download it directly from the form (you're already logged in on your own PC).
  1. On the order form, click "More information" next to Attach PDF (official template: hrs.isr.umich.edu/sites/default/files/HRS-Sensitive-Data-Access-Use-Agreement.pdf).
  2. Open it and complete the applicant fields — typically: name, title/role, institution/organization, address, email, the datasets requested, and your signature + date. Use the same name/entity as in Section 1.
  3. It is a self-signed researcher agreement (no separate institutional signing official is required for this Sensitive Health Data tier — unlike the dbGaP genotype track).
  4. Save the signed file as a PDF under 2 MB and attach it in the Attach PDF field.

Tip: if your PDF tool produces a file over 2 MB, "print to PDF" again at lower quality or flatten it.

4 · Reusable research-use justification

Paste a tightened version of this into the agreement's purpose field (and keep it consistent with anything you've told HRS before):

"HexaGene is a deterministic, sequence-intrinsic biophysical score that predicts missense-variant pathogenicity from local DNA thermodynamic and mechanical properties, validated against ClinVar/REVEL using conventional statistics. We will use HRS molecular, biomarker, aging, and health-outcome measures as independent reference phenotypes to validate and calibrate HexaGene-derived variant measures in a deeply phenotyped older-adult cohort. Data will be stored securely, restricted to authorized project personnel, never redistributed, and never used to re-identify or contact participants, in accordance with HRS terms."

5 · AI / LLM Use Policy — you comply (this is in your favor)

HRS's Conditions of Use (updated 02/2026) prohibit using LLMs and other AI tools to manage, process, or analyze HRS data. HexaGene is not affected: it is a deterministic, fixed-weight biophysical formula validated with classical statistics (t-tests, Pearson correlation, ROC-AUC). There is no machine-learning model, neural network, or LLM in the scoring or analysis pipeline — exactly the conventional statistical analysis the policy permits.
One tooling caveat (not about your method): do not run the actual downloaded HRS data through any LLM/AI assistant for analysis — that would breach the policy regardless of HexaGene. Keep analysis inside your scipy/HexaGene pipeline. (Using an assistant to fill out application forms is fine — that isn't processing HRS data.)
